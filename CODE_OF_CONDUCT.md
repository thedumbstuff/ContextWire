# Code of Conduct

ContextWire adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep
discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private
information are not tolerated in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's
**Security -> Report a vulnerability** form (private to maintainers) or by contacting a
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content
and restrict participation, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
